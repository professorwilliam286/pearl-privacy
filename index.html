Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Pearl Flix Uganda ("we," "our," or "the App") is developed and operated by Profesor. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your choices regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

a) Payment Information

When you subscribe to a paid plan, payment processing is handled by our third-party payment partner, Flutterwave. We may receive your name, email address, phone number, and transaction details (amount, date, plan selected) to activate and manage your subscription. We do not store your full card or mobile money credentials — these are processed directly by Flutterwave in accordance with their own privacy policy.

b) Advertising Data

The App displays ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID), IP address, and general usage data to serve and measure ads, which may include personalized advertising depending on your device and regional settings. You can review and manage ad personalization through your device's advertising settings. See Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy for details.

c) Device and Security Information

To protect our content and paying subscribers from unauthorized access and piracy, the App performs on-device checks for VPN usage, rooted devices, emulators, and network interception tools. These checks run locally on your device and evaluate device and network characteristics — they do not transmit personal browsing content or media consumption history to us.

d) Diagnostic and Crash Data

We use Google Firebase Crashlytics to automatically collect crash reports and diagnostic logs (such as device model, OS version, and app version) to help us identify and fix bugs. See Firebase's Privacy and Security policy for details.

e) App Usage Data

We may collect basic, non-identifying usage information (such as which categories or shows are viewed) to improve app performance and content offerings.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to: process and manage subscriptions; display and measure advertising; detect and prevent fraud, piracy, and unauthorized account sharing; diagnose and fix technical issues; and improve the App's features and content.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the third-party service providers named above (Flutterwave, Google AdMob, Google Firebase), each processing data under their own privacy policies, and only as necessary for them to provide their respective services to us.

4. Data Retention

We retain subscription and transaction records for as long as necessary to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes. Advertising and diagnostic data are retained according to Google's own retention policies.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

6. Your Choices

You may disable personalized advertising through your device's settings. You may request deletion of your subscription-related personal data by contacting us at the email below, subject to any records we are required to retain by law.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including the device security checks described above, which help prevent unauthorized access to paid content. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
